The Journey of Raj King

history_edu A Legacy of Excellence

From Tiny Sparks to Major Breakthroughs

Every big idea starts small. Raj King, founded by Mr. Abhi Patel in 2022, began with a humble vision: to provide premium quality food products at prices every family could afford.

Starting with just 15 bags per day from a small facility in Gondal, Rajkot, the brand has grown under dynamic leadership. Our commitment to the "crunchy start" and culinary expertise has transformed us from a local operation into an industrial leader.
